Our Story

Global expertise, brought home

The idea of Stratus Partners was conceived following discussions between our founders about the poor state of the Nigerian online banking space. One of us had tried to use a bank app, and for 3 days, there was downtime. This caused him to miss out on an opportunity.

Having lived in the United Kingdom for almost 2 decades and worked in IT consulting for top commercial banks, FinTechs, government institutions and multinationals for over a decade, we decided to bring our wealth of knowledge and experience back home.

Our mission is to implement technology solutions that enable African blue-chips to provide world-class services, and to halt the prevalence of downtimes and unreliable systems in their online operations.

"SPL was registered as an Africa-focused technology consulting company, with an objective to implement solutions that will address the erratic performance of IT infrastructure and mobile apps in Africa."
— Stratus Partners Founding Team

What We Do

Cloud & DevOps services for real-world teams

Our offerings follow two pillars – Consult & Architect and Build & Optimise – moving from assessment through implementation and continuous improvement.

Consult & Architect

DevOps Capability Assessment

A structured review of your DevOps practices across people, process, and tooling. Delivered in four phases: initial assessment, findings & recommendations, action plan, and progress tracking.

Consult & Architect

Cloud Principles & Foundations

Well-architected reviews, risk and security analysis, cost modelling, and target architectures implemented as code – creating a solid landing zone for product teams.

Build & Optimise

Infrastructure Design & Engineering

Building and deployment of automated self-healing infrastructure. When a server is down, another picks up immediately while the failed server self-repairs using historical data and insights.

Build & Optimise

CI/CD & Deployment Optimisation

Continuous Integration and Deployment pipelines that facilitate rapid delivery of code from developers to production without manual intervention, reducing errors and deployment cycles.

Build & Optimise

Product Re-Engineering

We remodel and modernise existing systems with APIs, containerization, and composability, to deliver efficient and adaptable platforms focused on your business needs.

Build & Optimise

Cloud Security & Compliance

Implementing Continuous Cloud Compliance Frameworks for preventing risks, detecting issues in real-time, correcting/remediating risk events, and managing exemptions.

Our Approach

Cloud principles & continuous improvement

We apply consistent cloud and DevOps principles – adapted pragmatically to your context – so platforms are sustainable and teams stay in control.

Well-Architected by Default

Regular reviews highlight operational risk, security gaps, and out-of-support technology, feeding into a prioritised improvement backlog.

Everything as Code

Infrastructure, pipelines, policies and dashboards defined as code using Terraform and cloud-native tooling for repeatability and governance.

Governance with Visibility

Centralised dashboards so leaders see impact in terms of risk, cost and customer experience – not just tickets and incidents.

Continuous Improvement

Regular retrospectives, Kaizen mindset and automation-first thinking rather than one-off "big bang" projects.
